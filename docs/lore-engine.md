# The Lore Engine

The Lore Engine represents the missing link in our architecture. Its core purpose is to serve as a machine-readable inference engine that empowers AI to connect the dots between factual gameplay data and expansive in-game lore. By grounding its reasoning in documented evidence, the engine is designed to synthesize insights and generate rich, accurate prose.

Currently, the biggest roadblock is the lack of a reliable, structured data source for quests, NPCs, and in-game books. If we are unable to secure a dependable source for this narrative data, our fallback strategy will be to eventually probe and extract it directly from the raw game files.
